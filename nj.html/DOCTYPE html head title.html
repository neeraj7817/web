LISTS ON HTML

HTML lists are fundamental structural elements used by developers to organize and group related information into clear, easily readable formats.

1. Unordered Lists

Used when the sequence of items does not matter, such as shopping lists or navigation menus

Example :
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Banana

2. Ordered Lists

Used when items follow a specific sequence, such as recipes or step-by-step guides.

Example:
  1. Egg
  2. Milk
  3. Banana

3. Description Lists

Used for name-value pairs, glossaries, or dictionary terms

Example:
HTML
HyperText Markup Language
MCA
Master of computer application
BCA
Bachelor of computer application
