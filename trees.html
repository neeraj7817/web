Trees are vital, long-lived woody plants that form the backbone of life on Earth. Composed of roots, a strong trunk, spreading branches, and leaves, they perform photosynthesis to turn sunlight and carbon dioxide into the oxygen we breathe.They act as natural carbon sinks, cool the air, and protect soil from erosion. Beyond their environmental role, trees provide food, medicine, wood, and shelter for countless animals and humans.Protecting and planting trees is essential to keep our planet green, healthy, and balanced for future generations
