Fruits are the sweet and edible parts of flowering plants that grow from flowers and contain seeds. They come in many bright colors, shapes, and sizes, such as apples, bananas, oranges, and mangoes. Eating fruits is very good for your health. They are naturally sweet, low in fat and calories, and full of important vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber. Regular intake helps boost your immune system, gives your body natural energy, and keeps your digestion strong. You can enjoy them fresh, dried, or blended into juices every day.
