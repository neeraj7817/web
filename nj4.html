INDIAN FESTIVALS

  • Onam

    • Onam is the official 10-day state and harvest festival of Kerala Tourism that celebrates the Malayali New Year and the legendary annual homecoming of the benevolent King Mahabali

  • Diwali

    • Diwali is a joyful Hindu festival of lights that celebrates the victory of good over evil

  • Holi

    • Holi is a famous and joyful festival of colors celebrated mainly in India to mark the arrival of spring
