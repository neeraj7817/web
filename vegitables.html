Vegetables are the edible parts of plants, such as roots, stems, leaves, and flowers. They are a vital part of a healthy daily diet. Most vegetables are low in calories and high in water, vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber. Eating them helps keep your body strong, improves digestion, and protects you from many diseases. You can eat vegetables raw in salads, or cook them in soups, stews, and stir-fries. Adding different colorful vegetables to your plate every day gives your body the energy and nutrients it needs to stay active and well.
